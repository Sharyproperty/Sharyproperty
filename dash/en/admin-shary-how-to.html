How to use the dashboard

A written, step-by-step guide to everything done from here. Pick what you want to do, follow the steps, and press the button to open the screen.

No section mentions this word — try another one, or open "Where do I edit what?".

1 — Start here: the shape of the dashboardThe things you will use every day.
Steps
  1. The menu on the side is grouped: daily work (requests, units, projects, developers, areas) → content and website → the app → administration. Press a group to open it.
  2. The search box at the top looks everywhere: a project, developer, area, unit, article or a screen name. Type a few letters and pick a result.
  3. Above every table there is "Search this list" — type to reach the row you want.
  4. Every list of choices (developer, project, area…) opens with a search box — type the name instead of scrolling.
  5. The "English / عربي" button switches the dashboard language. The arrow beside it opens the website.
  6. Under the title of every screen, "This screen controls…" tells you what it changes on the website.
  • 💡 Cannot find where something is? Open "Where do I edit what?" — everything shown on the website and the screen that edits it.

2 — Add a unit (developer / resale / rent)A unit is any apartment, villa or chalet offered for sale or rent.
Steps
  1. Open Units → Add a unit.
  2. Choose the sale type first: developer (primary), resale or rent — the fields below change with it.
  3. Type the Arabic and English names, then choose the area, the project and the property type (every list has a search).
  4. Type the size, rooms, bathrooms and the price (EGP or USD).
  5. Payment plans (developer units): add one or more — down payment, years, instalment and how often. Cash is a plan of its own.
  6. Upload the photos (the first one is the main photo), then press save.
  7. The "Price" tab: type the price and choose the currency (EGP / USD) — for developer, resale and rent units. For rent also choose "Rent per" (day / week / month / 6 months / year) and the "Deposit value" (no deposit / 1 month / 2 months / 3 months…) — both show on the card and inside the unit page.
  8. After saving, open "Unit page data" for more details (rent: daily / monthly / yearly, furnished…).
  • 💡 A unit shows on the website only when its status is "active".
  • 💡 The address of the unit (slug) does not change after saving — so search ranking is not affected.

3 — Upload many units at once (Excel sheet)For 20 or 200 units in one project, upload them in one file.
Steps
  1. Open Units → Upload a units sheet.
  2. Choose the project (with the search).
  3. Choose the kind of units: developer / resale / rent — each kind has its own template and columns.
  4. Download the template and fill it: one row per unit. Do not rename the columns.
  5. Upload the file and press upload. The units are added to the chosen project.
  6. The "currency" column takes EGP / USD (جنيه / دولار); the rent sheet has "deposit_months" for the deposit (1 / 2 / 3 months).
  • 💡 Try two or three rows first to make sure the template is right.
Open: Upload a units sheet
4 — Add a project and its page dataA project (compound) is added once; its units go inside it.
Steps
  1. Open Projects → Add a project. Type the name, choose the developer and the area, upload the photos and the master plan.
  2. After saving, open "Project data for the website" and pick the project: description, payment plans, amenities, FAQs, SEO.
  3. To show it in "New launches" or "Top projects" on the home page: the "Website home page" group.
  4. The project boundary on the map: "Project boundaries on the map".
  • 💡 While a project is open, tabs appear at the top: basics, photos, website data, units… and "open the page on the website".
Open: Add a projectOpen: All projectsOpen: Project data for the website
5 — DevelopersAdding a developer, the data of its page, and its sales contact.
Steps
  1. Open Developers → All developers (add and edit): name, logo, description.
  2. Open "Developer page data" and pick the developer: short name, founding year, numbers, the Shary index of the developer, FAQs.
  3. On the same screen, the "Developer sales" card: the name, WhatsApp number and e-mail of the sales contact — the person who receives the meeting link when a customer books "a meeting with the developer's sales" in Shary AI.
  4. The order of developers on the home page and the "show all" page: "Top developers and the show-all page".
  • 💡 The sales contact never appears on the website.
Open: All developersOpen: Developer page dataOpen: Top developers
6 — Main areas and sub-areasA main area (North Coast, Red Sea…) with its areas inside (Ras El Hekma, El Gouna…). Any number, no limit.
Steps
  1. Open Areas → All areas and sub-areas, and choose the country.
  2. A new main area: the top card "+ Add a main area" — pick an existing area, or type a new name and press "Add it".
  3. Inside the card of the main area: tick the areas inside it and save, or type the name of "a new sub-area" and press "Add the sub-area".
  4. The photo, description, SEO and FAQs of an area: the "Area page data" button beside its name.
  • 💡 One level only: a sub-area has no areas of its own.
Open: Main areas and sub-areasOpen: Area page data
7 — Owners' requests: sell / rent out (review and publish)When a customer fills "list your property" on the website, the request arrives here with everything he sent.
Steps
  1. Open Requests and messages → Sell requests (or Rent requests).
  2. Every request is a card: the customer and his number (call and WhatsApp), the price (cash, or cash + instalments, or the rent), the property, and the photos and videos he uploaded — press a photo to enlarge it.
  3. All good? Press "Publish on the website" — the unit goes live with the customer's photos.
  4. Want to change something first (name, price, description, more photos)? Press "Review and edit before publishing" — the unit form opens filled from the request.
  5. Not suitable? "Reject". A published request can be un-published.
  • 💡 The owner's number is not written on the listing — a visitor contacts Shary.
  • 💡 When "Publish" is not shown, the card says what the request is missing.
Open: Sell requestsOpen: Rent requests
8 — All requests, messages and e-mailsEvery form of the website (meeting, consultation, contact, Shary AI, Shary Card, careers) is saved in one inbox and sends an e-mail.
Steps
  1. Open Requests and messages → All requests. The number beside it is the count of new requests.
  2. Open a request to see all its details, and change its status (new / contacted / done).
  3. The e-mail: one complete e-mail per request with the customer's name, number and e-mail, everything he wrote, the date and time, the request number and a button that opens the request here. "Reply" goes to the customer.
  • 💡 The address that receives the requests is set on the server (the developer) — by default the e-mail of "Contact details".
Open: All requestsOpen: Contact messages
9 — The website home pageEverything shown on the home page has a screen in the "Website home page" group.
Steps
  1. Header photos (desktop and phone) and the slider: "Header photos" and "Slider".
  2. The five cards (Exclusive offers / Egypt map / Sahel map / Shary Card / Trending): "Home cards" — title, button and link; upload a new design from "Pictures of the card" when you have one.
  3. The "What sets us apart" cards (Shary Ma3ak, Shary Academy…): "What sets us apart" — icon, name and link.
  4. New launches, top units / projects / areas, most wanted areas: each has its screen in the same group.
  5. Ads and the "most searched" links of the footer: the same group.
  • 💡 Changed something and it does not show on the website? Open SEO and redirects → Refresh the website.

10 — Writing articles and any content (the editor)The same editor in every content box: articles, About us, Terms, the description of a project, an area or a developer.
Steps
  1. A new article: Articles and content → Blog and the writing editor.
  2. A row of named buttons sits above the editor: "Heading" and "Sub-heading" for titles, "Normal text" for a paragraph.
  3. "Bullets", "Numbers" and "Arrow list" for lists — type a line and press Enter for the next, Enter twice to leave the list.
  4. "Divider" puts a line between two parts, "Space" adds a gap.
  5. The symbol buttons (، ؟ — • ←) type the symbol at the cursor.
  6. "Call to action" puts a contact block inside the text (book a meeting / call / WhatsApp); "Internal link" links the selected words to a project, an area or an article.
  7. Press "Preview" to see the text as the website prints it (desktop and phone), then save.
  • 💡 The big page title is typed in the title field — inside the editor use "Heading" and "Sub-heading" only.

11 — Shary AI and meetingsThe assistant that answers customers on the website, and booking a meeting inside it.
Steps
  1. Open Shary AI: the connection status at the top, then "Try it" — type a question and see the answer a customer gets.
  2. The tone of the answers and customer words (Tagamoa = New Cairo…): the same screen.
  3. The "Meetings from the chat" card: whether Zoom and WhatsApp are connected, the "meeting with the developer's sales" switch and the meeting length.
  4. At the bottom: customers' questions — the ones that found nothing are shown separately.
  • 💡 The AI, Zoom and WhatsApp keys live on the server only (the developer) — never typed here or in any chat.
Open: Shary AI
12 — The agent (the assistant that works with you)It writes articles, completes pages that miss something, reviews customers' requests and analyses the market every day — everything waits for your approval. It never deletes and never publishes by itself.
Steps
  1. "Today": what was done, what waits for your decision, and the main findings of the day's analysis.
  2. "Review box": every proposal — open it, see before and after, then publish, edit, ask for a better result or cancel.
  3. "Evaluate the agent's work" (under every proposal): good / needs to be better + a remark — the agent follows your remarks in its next work.
  4. "Commands": type a request in plain words (write an article about…, update the description of…, analyse an area…) or attach a sheet and photos.
  5. "Analysis": daily, weekly and monthly — the market, areas, projects, developers, prices and Shary against the competitors, what changed since the day / week / month before, with a button for what to do beside each finding. Every analysis has "Save as PDF" and "Share the PDF on WhatsApp".
  6. "Reports": a report every day — "Save as PDF", "Share the PDF on WhatsApp" (makes the file and opens the share list — pick WhatsApp), "Send a WhatsApp message", "Email it" or "Copy". Under "Where does the daily report reach you?" you type the email and the WhatsApp number.
  7. "Daily jobs": article of the day, the daily, weekly and monthly analysis, competitors' news, developers' numbers, completing pages… any job can be paused or moved to another time.
  8. "Pages that miss something": every page and what is empty in it, with "let the agent complete it".
  9. "Log and undo": anything published can be taken back.
  • 💡 The agent never changes page addresses, never deletes anything and never publishes without approval.
  • 💡 Writing and Google search need keys on the server (the developer) — the analysis, the reports and the review of requests work without them.

13 — SEO, redirects and refreshing the websiteGoogle titles and descriptions, and sending an old address to a new one.
Steps
  1. The Google title and description of a page: the "page data" of the project / area / developer / unit / article — the SEO fields.
  2. An old address changed? SEO and redirects → Redirects: type the old and the new address.
  3. Edited something and it does not show? SEO and redirects → Refresh the website.
  • 💡 Existing page addresses are not changed unless necessary — and then with a redirect.
Open: RedirectsOpen: Refresh the website
14 — Users and permissionsWho enters the dashboard and what they see.
Steps
  1. Administration → Users and permissions → Supervisors: add a supervisor and choose the sections he sees.
  2. A supervisor of one developer sees that developer's projects only.
  3. Customers and agents registered on the website and the app: the same group.
  • 💡 Everyone signs in with his own account — do not share a password.
