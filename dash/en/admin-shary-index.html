Shary Index

All Shary Index numbers from one place: area figures here, and monthly prices, project indicators, developer index and the page texts from the tabs.
  • 1. Area figures
  • 2. Monthly prices
  • 3. Project indicators
  • 4. Developer index
  • 5. Index page texts
Where each number appears
Price per m² (EGP)Shary Index page, the index card on the area page, the index strip in the header and home page, and the "average price vs area" comparison on project and unit pages.
Annual growth %The increase next to the price per metre in the index and header strip, and the "growth rate" of projects without their own value.
Demand (out of 100)"Demand index" on the index and area pages, and on projects and units without their own value.
Area index (out of 100)The Shary Index score of the area on the index page and the area card.
Rental yield %"Rental yield" on the index and area pages, and the "rental index / rental income" of projects and units without their own value.
ROI %The "ROI" figure in the area numbers. (The badge next to the unit price is now the "annual growth rate" and comes from the growth field.)
Investment ratingThe area rating (high / medium / low) on the index page and "top investment areas".
An empty cell = the website calculates the value from the monthly prices or from the units and projects of the area. The chart and monthly change come from the "Monthly prices" tab.
