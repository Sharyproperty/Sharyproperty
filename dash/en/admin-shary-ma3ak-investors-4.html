Account control
Secret code (PIN)

The investor has a secret code. If he forgot it or it is locked, reset it and the app asks him to create a new one.

Change the mobile number (support)

For an investor who lost the old number and cannot receive its code in the app. First make sure this is the account holder (identity documents, a call), then type the new number.

The new number is saved as verified at once, "Forgot PIN" stays closed for 24 hours after it, and the change is written in the activity log.

Block the account

A blocked account cannot buy shares, top up or withdraw from the wallet in the app. His current investments and installments stay as they are.
