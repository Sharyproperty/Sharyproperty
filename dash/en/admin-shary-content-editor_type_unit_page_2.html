The unit description on the unit page.
Show the ready structure automatically on: |
A page whose text is not written yet shows by itself the same structure as the design (built from the real projects, prices and areas). As soon as you write the text and save it, your text takes its place. Untick to keep the page without text until you write it. The sentences of the structure are edited in “Page texts and images → Ready structure of the articles”. The uploaded ad images (Ads screen) appear in the home page banners. Tick this to show them as well after the cards of the ad box inside the search, area and blog pages.
Contact buttons inside the content:
A "Request a meeting / Call / WhatsApp" block is placed inside any long content: area article, blog, project description, unit, developer and news — after the first section, then every N sections. Codes typed by the writer ([shary-cta]) count toward the total. The three titles rotate in order; leave a field empty for the default title and write :name where the area / project name goes.
Areas outside Egypt:
Hidden by default: areas and projects of other countries do not appear on the interactive map or in the area strip of "New launches / Top projects". The "Properties abroad" pages and their links keep working.
Design content:
The content written with the approved design: 8 blog articles (headings, structure, internal links, FAQs and images), the lists of famous universities, restaurants, schools and leisure places of the main areas, and the internal-link keywords that point at them. Additive only: nothing existing is changed or removed, and afterwards everything is edited from its own screen (articles here, places under "Famous places", keywords under "Internal links"). The texts of the three showcase pages (New Cairo opening paragraphs, guide and FAQs, the Scenes project article and the Mountain View story) are written only into empty fields.
