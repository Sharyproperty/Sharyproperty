Unit type
The type can be changed at any time — the unit moves to its section on the site (for sale / for rent) and its old link redirects to the new one.

Specs
The small tag on the card photo (e.g. garden view).
Amenities
When nothing is chosen and the unit belongs to a project, the project amenities are shown.
Price
Shown on the unit card and inside its page: "Deposit: 2 months".
Main payment planResale unit payment
The numbers shown on the unit card ("instalment / years"). For more plans and offers use the Offers section below.
All cash = the price only. Cash + installments = the cash needed now, the rest in installments (quarterly / semi-annual / annual …).
%
Payment plan details: extra down payments + months between payments
"Down payment 1" is the down payment field of the plan itself. Add down payment 2, 3 (or more) per plan here, and enter the number of months between payments (e.g. 3 or 6). The instalments total and the delivery payment are calculated automatically from the plan data and are not typed.
If the unit has more than one payment plan (like the old dashboard): add one plan per row — the first plan is the one above.
Offers and payment plans
Add several offers or payment plans for the same unit without duplicating it. Each offer shows in the "Payment plans" section of the unit page in this order. "Hidden" = saved but not shown on the site.

Discounted resale opportunity ("Resale opportunities" page)
Different from the offers above: it lists the unit on the resale opportunities page with a discount and an end date.
Photos
The card photo. JPG / PNG / WebP up to 6 MB — 1600×1067 (3:2) recommended.
Optional — a picture or a PDF. When empty the project master plan is used.
Several photos can be chosen at once (up to 30). Photos are shown whole, without zoom.
A link on YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram or anywhere — the "video" button of the unit page opens it where it is (outside the website).
Floor plans
Upload an image for every plan and name it (e.g. ground floor).
Location
Optional. A unit that belongs to a project takes the project position when the coordinates are empty.
Unit description
Shown in the "Description" section of the unit page. Use the editor buttons to add a call-to-action or an internal link inside the text.

The questions on a unit page come from its project.

SEO and link
