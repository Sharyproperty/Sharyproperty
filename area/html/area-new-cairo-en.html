About New Cairo

About the city

New Cairo is one of the largest new cities in East Cairo. It combines quiet living with services close by: universities, international schools, malls, and main roads that take you to central Cairo and the New Administrative Capital in a short time.

The city has districts with different characters: Fifth Settlement for services and malls, Mostakbal City for newer projects at lower prices, and El Shorouk and Madinaty for quiet and larger spaces.

Below you will find everything you need to know before buying: location and roads, districts, unit types, prices, payment plans, developers and services.

  • Universities and international schools
  • Malls and leisure
  • Hospitals and medical centers
  • Clubs and green space
  • Main roads and axes
  • Close to the New Capital

How much does location affect the price?

15 min to the airport

The city sits on Cairo’s eastern edge, between Suez Road to the north and the Ring Road to the west, which makes it a midpoint between old Cairo and the New Administrative Capital.

Projects on the main roads sell for up to 20% more per meter, because travel time is shorter and the unit is easier to resell or rent later.

Units away from the main roads are cheaper and larger for the same budget, and suit buyers who plan to live there more than investors.

1.Districts and areas of New Cairo

The city is divided into settlements and districts, each with its own character and price. Fifth Settlement is the most expensive and best served, and Mostakbal City is growing fastest.

2.Unit types and sizes

From a 55 m² studio to a standalone villa above 400 m². Apartments are the most in demand, and the townhouse is the middle choice between an apartment and a villa.

3.Property prices and average price per meter

Prices move with the area, delivery stage and finishing. The figures above are an approximate average of the units available on Shary.

4.Payment and installment plans

Most developers start from 5% to 20% down with installments over 6 to 10 years. The lower the down payment, the higher the total price, so compare the final cost and not the down payment alone.

5.Best-known developers in the area

A developer with a strong reputation lowers the risk of delay and affects the resale price. Check the developer’s delivery record, not just the brand.

6.Schools, universities and services

The factor that matters most to buyers with children. Being close to universities also raises rental demand all year round.

7.Malls and leisure

The largest cluster of malls in East Cairo, which makes nearby units higher in price and faster to rent.

8.Investment and rental yield

The annual yield is usually between 6% and 9% depending on location and finishing. Small units near universities rent fastest, and larger ones gain more in price.

Frequently asked questions about New Cairo

What is the difference between New Cairo and Fifth Settlement?

Fifth Settlement is part of New Cairo. It is the city’s best-known district and the highest in average price and services.

How big is New Cairo?

It is one of the largest new cities in East Cairo and includes many settlements and districts of different sizes.

Is Madinaty part of New Cairo?

Madinaty is very close and counts as part of East Cairo. We show it here because many buyers compare the two.

What is the average price per meter in New Cairo?

The residential average is about EGP 78,400 per meter, and it varies by district and delivery stage.

What is the best payment plan available now?

There are offers starting from 5% down with installments up to 8 years. Contact us and we will suggest what fits your budget.
