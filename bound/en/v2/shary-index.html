    How is the Shary Index calculated?

    Index for

    The area index is a score out of 100 built from 4 factors for the market average in the selected area and unit type.

    Liquidity and sales speed 30%
    Price growth 25%
    Area growth 25%
    Services and facilities 20%

    The project index is a score out of 100 from 5 equally weighted factors — every unit inside a project uses its project's calculation.

    Average price vs. the area 20%
    Project price growth 20%
    Area growth 20%
    On-time delivery 20%
    Services and facilities 20%

    The developer index is a score out of 100 from 8 factors that measure the developer's record across all projects.

    Liquidity and selling speed 20%
    Price growth after delivery 20%
    On-time delivery 15%
    Financial solvency 15%
    Developer growth 10%
    Product quality 10%
    Services and facilities 5%
    Service and support 5%

    The index is a guide for reading the market, not a substitute for viewing the unit and reviewing the contract.

    Frequently asked questions about the index

    How is the Shary Index calculated?

    From 4 factors: liquidity and sales speed, price growth, area growth, and services and facilities. Each factor has a weight and the total is a score out of 100.

    How often are the numbers updated?

    Every month, from deals, supply and demand data on Shary.

    Is the index price the actual selling price?

    It is the average listed and sold price per meter in the area for the selected unit type, so a specific unit can differ by developer, finishing and delivery date.

    How do I use the index before buying?

    Compare the price per meter of the unit you like with its area average, check the price and demand trend over the last 12 months, then ask Shary for advice.
