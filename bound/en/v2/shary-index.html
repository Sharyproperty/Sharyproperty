    How is the Shary Index calculated?

    [Demo] The area index is a score out of 100 built from these factors.

    Price growth 40%
    Demand level 35%
    Rental yield 25%

    The index is a guide for reading the market, not a substitute for viewing the unit and reviewing the contract.

    Frequently asked questions about the index

    [Demo] What is the Shary Index?

    Demo text for the test bed: the Shary Index shows the average price per m² and demand level across Egypt from Shary figures and listed units.

    [Demo] How often are the figures updated?

    Demo text: monthly figures are entered in the dashboard, and unit averages are computed from the published units.
