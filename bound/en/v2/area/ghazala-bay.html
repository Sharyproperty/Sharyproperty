What makes a project one of the top in the area?

The ranking is not based on advertising but on four things we measure from market data: the developer’s record of delivering on time, the location relative to main roads and services, the share sold in earlier phases, and resale activity.

These projects are usually higher per meter than others in the same area. The difference buys a lower risk of delay and more liquidity if you decide to sell the unit.

1.Projects and phases in Ghazala Bay

Each project in Ghazala Bay has its own character and price depending on the developer and delivery stage. Compare nearby projects before you choose.

2.Unit types and sizes

From studios and small apartments to standalone villas. Apartments are the most in demand, and the townhouse is the middle choice between an apartment and a villa.

3.Property prices and average price per meter

Prices move with the project, delivery stage and finishing. The figures above are an approximate average of the units available on Shary.

4.Payment and installment plans

Most developers start from 5% to 20% down with installments over 6 to 10 years. The lower the down payment, the higher the total price, so compare the final cost and not the down payment alone.

5.Best-known developers in the area

A developer with a strong reputation lowers the risk of delay and affects the resale price. Check the developer’s delivery record, not just the brand.

6.Schools, universities and services

The factor that matters most to buyers with children. Being close to universities also raises rental demand all year round.

7.Malls and leisure

Being close to malls and leisure makes a unit higher in price and faster to rent.

8.Investment and rental yield

The yield varies with location and finishing. Small units rent fastest, and larger ones gain more in price.
