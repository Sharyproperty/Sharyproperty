Shary Card

The Shary discount and rewards card: a discount when you buy through us, and a reward when you refer someone.

What is Shary Card?

Shary Card is a card in your name with your own code, issued to you by Shary. When you buy your property through Shary and sign the contract, the code gives you a special discount. And when someone you refer buys through us, you earn your reward as a Shary Ambassador, and they get a Shary Card with their own discount too.

What benefits can you get?

  • Shary Card - 2% cashback
  • A special discount after signing the contract when you buy through Shary
  • A reward as a Shary Ambassador for every client you refer who buys
  • The person you refer gets a Shary Card in their name with their own discount
  • Exclusive offers from our partners

Shary Ambassador

Your code is not just for you: refer family and friends, and earn with everyone who buys through Shary.

  1. 1 Share your code Send your Shary Card code to anyone looking for a property.
  2. 2 Your friend buys They buy through Shary with the code, sign the contract and get a Shary Card with their discount.
  3. 3 You get rewarded Your Shary Ambassador reward is recorded after the contract is signed.

How do you get a Shary Card?

  1. 1 Enter your name and phone number in the form.
  2. 2 Shary issues a card in your name with your own code.
  3. 3 Give the code to your Shary advisor when you buy — the discount applies after signing the contract.
