Resale or buying from the developer: when is resale the better deal?

Shary Team · Last updated 22 July 2026

Many buyers are torn between resale and buying directly from the developer. Resale can give you a ready unit at a good price, while the developer offers a lower down payment and longer installments. This article compares the two and explains when resale is the better decision.

What is resale?

Resale means buying a unit from its current owner rather than from the developer. The unit may be delivered and fully paid, or still on installments, in which case the seller assigns the contract to the buyer at the developer’s office and receives what was paid plus an amount known as the over price.

Resale vs buying from the developer

PointResaleFrom the developer
Upfront amountLarger: what the seller paid plus the over priceA small down payment, usually 5% to 10%
DeliveryImmediate or soonYears away in new projects
ViewingYou see the unit and the project for realYou rely on plans and models
NegotiationRoom to negotiate with the ownerSet prices and offers
Installment periodOnly what remains of the seller’s planA full plan of up to 10 years

When is resale better?

  • When you want to move in soon and cannot wait years for delivery.
  • When you have substantial cash and want a price below the developer’s current launch.
  • When you want a prime location that sold out in the first launch.
  • When you prefer to see the unit, the neighbours and the services before paying.

Steps to buy a resale unit safely

  1. Ask for the original contract and payment receipts, and check the unit and owner details.
  2. Check with the developer that there are no late installments, penalties or unpaid maintenance deposits.
  3. Learn the assignment conditions and fees, and complete the assignment at the developer’s office, not only through a private contract.
  4. Inspect the unit and compare the total price with the area’s average price per meter.

Frequently asked questions about resale units

What does “over price” mean?

It is the amount the buyer pays the current owner on top of what the owner has paid the developer, reflecting how much the unit has gained in value since it was contracted.

Can I buy a resale unit in installments?

Yes: you pay what the seller has paid plus the over price in cash, then continue the remaining installments to the developer after the assignment.

Who pays the assignment fees?

It is agreed between seller and buyer, and the developer collects the fees as set in the original contract.
