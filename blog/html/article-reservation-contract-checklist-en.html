7 clauses to review in a unit reservation contract before signing

Shary Team · Last updated 29 July 2026

A unit reservation contract is the first paper you sign with the developer, and your rights until delivery rest on it. Many disputes start from a clause that was not read carefully or wording that was unclear. Before you sign, review the seven clauses below and ask for written clarification of anything vague.

Reservation form vs preliminary sale contract

A reservation form records your intent to buy and holds the unit for a set period against a reservation payment. The preliminary sale contract is the binding agreement with every detail: price, payment schedule, delivery date and specifications. The clauses below apply to both.

The seven clauses to review

  1. Unit details Unit number, floor and building, the net and gross areas and the load factor.
  2. Price and payment plan The total price, the installment schedule with dates, the maintenance deposit and any extra fees such as parking, club and meters.
  3. Delivery date The handover date, the grace period and the delay penalty the developer accepts.
  4. Finishing specifications An annex that sets the delivery condition, finishing type and materials, not general phrases.
  5. Cancellation and refunds The share deducted if you withdraw, how long refunds take, and what happens if an installment is late.
  6. Assignment and resale The conditions for assigning the unit to someone else and the fees the company charges.
  7. Ownership and licences The land title, the ministerial decree and the building permit, and the developer’s commitment to transfer ownership and register the unit.

Before you sign

  • Ask for a copy of the contract to read calmly, and do not sign in the same meeting.
  • Check the unit details in the contract against the approved plan.
  • Keep receipts for every payment and a signed copy of the contract.
  • Use a lawyer who specialises in property contracts when a clause is unclear.

Frequently asked questions about unit reservation contracts

Is the reservation payment refundable?

It depends on the reservation form: the refund period and the share the company deducts if you withdraw. Read that clause before paying.

What is the load factor?

It is the unit’s share of common areas such as stairs and entrances, added to the net area to give the gross area the price is based on.

Can contract clauses be changed?

You can ask for an annex or a clarification of unclear clauses before signing, and it is best to have a specialised lawyer review the contract.
