The Egyptian real estate market is one of the most active in the region, and its prices and payment plans change from one period to the next. Before you decide to buy you need a calm reading of the market: which indicators deserve attention, what moves prices, and which areas fit your goal. This guide gives you a practical way to read the market in 2026.

Indicators that help you read the market

  • Average price per meter A reference for comparing areas, and units within the same area.
  • Price direction Whether the average has risen or held steady over recent quarters matters more than the number itself.
  • Supply Many projects on offer in one area give the buyer more room to negotiate.
  • Payment plans Installment periods and down payments reflect how hard developers are competing.
  • Build and delivery rates Areas where projects are delivered on time see stronger resale and rental demand.

What moves property prices in Egypt?

Prices respond to several factors working together, mainly:

  • Construction cost Building material prices and the exchange rate feed directly into the cost of new projects.
  • Interest rates They affect the cost of mortgage finance and how attractive property is as a store of savings.
  • Infrastructure New roads, axes and transport links raise the value of the areas they serve.
  • New cities The state’s expansion into new cities adds supply and creates new centres of demand.

Tips before you decide to buy

  1. Set your goal first: a home, rental income or resale. Each one points to a different area and unit type.
  2. Compare the unit’s price per meter with the area average, not with a single project.
  3. Review the developer’s track record and delivery history.
  4. Work out the total you will pay, not just the installment.
  5. Leave room in your budget for finishing, maintenance and registration costs.
