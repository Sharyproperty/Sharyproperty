The price per meter in New Cairo is the first thing buyers in East Cairo look at, as the city combines upscale housing with business districts and universities. This report shows the average price per meter in New Cairo’s main areas in the third quarter of 2026, based on Shary Index data, and the factors behind the differences between areas.

A look at the New Cairo market

New Cairo lies east of Cairo and includes the First, Third and Fifth Settlements. North and South 90th Street run through it, lined with offices and shopping centres. The city is home to the American University in Cairo, the German University in Cairo and Future University, and the East Nile monorail passes through it on the way to the New Administrative Capital, which supports demand for housing and rentals.

What affects the price per meter

  • Location Being close to 90th Street, the main axes and the universities raises the price.
  • Inside or outside a compound Compound units cost more for the security and services they offer.
  • Unit condition Full finishing and immediate delivery add to the price compared with a unit under construction.
  • Payment plan The cash price is lower than the long-installment price for the same unit.
  • Developer Track record and on-time delivery show in the price and in resale value.

How to read the numbers

The average is a guide, not the price of every unit. When comparing, look at units that match in size, floor, finishing and delivery date, and follow the price direction over more than one quarter before judging an area as expensive or cheap.
