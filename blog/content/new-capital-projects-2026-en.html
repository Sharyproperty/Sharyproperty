New Capital projects range from residential compounds to office towers, shopping centres and medical buildings, and developers compete for buyers with long payment plans. This article reviews the types of projects on offer in 2026 by area, the common payment plans, and how to compare offers before you reserve.

Types of projects in the New Capital

  • Residential compounds Concentrated in districts R7 and R8, with apartments, duplexes and villas in a range of sizes.
  • Central Business District towers Office, hotel and residential towers around the Iconic Tower.
  • Downtown projects Retail, office and medical buildings with small units that suit investors.
  • Green River projects Towers and units directly overlooking the Green River parks.

Well-known residential projects

The residential projects buyers ask about most include Al Maqsad in R3, Midtown, IL Bosco and The Capital Way in R7, La Vista City and Celia. Build and delivery stages differ from one project to another, so check each project’s current status before you reserve.

Common payment plans in 2026

Payment planHow it worksBest for
Down payment and equal installments5% to 10% down and the rest in quarterly installmentsBuyers with a steady income
Delivery paymentLower installments with a larger payment on deliveryBuyers expecting future liquidity
Long installmentsUp to 8 or 10 years at a higher total priceBuyers who want the lowest installment
Cash discountA discount on the price for paying upfrontBuyers who have the full amount

How to compare offers

  1. Compare the total unit price under each plan, not just the installment.
  2. Ask about the maintenance deposit, when it is due, and parking and club fees.
  3. Check actual build progress and the delivery date written in the contract.
  4. Compare the price per meter with the district average on the Shary Index.
