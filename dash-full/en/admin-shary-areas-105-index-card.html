Prices & index — سيدي حنيش

Every figure of the area in one place: its categories with price per m² from / to and their figures for each month, its general figures and its card — they show on the area page and the Shary Index right away.
Open the page on the website
Prices and figures of the area's categories Area categories: 1

The area's categories: under the North Coast, Sokhna or the Red Sea «Coastal» only, any other area Residential / Commercial / Administrative / Medical / Hotel. Pick the month and enter, for each category, the average price per m², the range from / to and its other figures — each category is saved on its own and empty fields are not changed. The latest month is what the site shows.

Untick «Show on the website» on any category to hide it for this area on the Shary Index and the area card — its figures stay saved; tick it again to bring it back.
