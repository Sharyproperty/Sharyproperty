About Scenes

Scenes in Mostakbal City is more than a price and a payment plan. It is developed by Tatweer Misr, a developer with a clear presence in East Cairo, and it sits on an axis linking New Cairo with the New Capital and Madinaty.

Prices start from EGP 27,160,000 with a 5% down payment and installments up to 10 years, with townhouses, twin houses and standalone villas. It suits long-term living as well as buyers thinking about resale value later.

Where exactly is Scenes?

Scenes is in Mostakbal City, East Cairo, close to New Cairo and the New Capital, with direct access to Suez Road, the Middle Ring Road and Al Amal Axis — keeping work, schools and daily services within reach.

Key places near Scenes

Being close to several key destinations makes the location practical for families, employees and students:

  • The New Administrative Capital is a short drive away, supporting the project's value as business moves east.
  • International schools and universities in New Cairo are nearby.
  • The project neighbours Madinaty and other established communities, so it is inside an active urban area.
  • The Middle Ring Road and Al Amal Axis give more than one route for the daily commute.

Scenes design

The project is planned as an open residential community, not tightly packed buildings: a low built-up ratio and larger open spaces, which shows in privacy and movement inside the compound.

The design relies on landscape, artificial lakes and open paths, with units overlooking green and water areas. Restaurants, walking areas and seating are distributed between the residential phases.

Project area

The land area places Scenes among the large residential projects in East Cairo, giving the developer room to spread phases, services and internal roads.

Facilities and services in Scenes

The project offers a network of services covering education, health, sports, leisure and security, spread across the phases so residents do not travel far inside the compound.

  • Smart home system in selected units.
  • Schools and a nursery within the services plan.
  • A commercial mall and shopping areas.
  • Green areas, artificial lakes and landscape in every phase.
  • Sports courts, a social club, walking and cycling tracks.
  • Security, parking, maintenance and cleaning services around the clock.

Why invest in Scenes

The opportunity does not rest on one factor: location, size, developer and internal services together.

  • A clear starting price with a low down payment and long installments gives more room to manage liquidity.
  • Urban growth around the project supports asset value: the area between New Cairo, the New Capital and Mostakbal City is growing fast.
  • Demand in East Cairo is now for complete, liveable communities, not just a unit.

The developer

Scenes is by Tatweer Misr, a developer with delivered projects in several areas, which helps buyers judge its ability to execute and deliver on time.

Tatweer Misr track record

  • Residential projects in Mostakbal City and New Cairo.
  • Coastal projects on the North Coast and Ain Sokhna.
  • Mixed-use projects (residential, commercial and offices).
